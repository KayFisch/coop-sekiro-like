# Attack pool

Attacks we've built or thought of that belong to no boss yet, each with a note on who might
suit them. A boss takes from here only what fits its identity (`docs/concept.md`, "Few attacks,
a distinct boss").

- **Rush, hop back / retreat** (changing place, making distance): the Acrobat.
- **Shove, pushing both apart:** maybe the Heavy Hitter, if he needs a way to make room.
- **Upward cut** (a cut from below, he runs under the player; the second crossing idea from the
  scribble of 2026-10-02): open.
- **Long strike series (LONG), SINGLE, TRIPLE, QUAD as patterns of their own:** try-out boss
  only.
